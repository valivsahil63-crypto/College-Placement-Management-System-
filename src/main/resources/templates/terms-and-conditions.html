Terms and Conditions

Effective: Academic Session 2024-2026 | Training and Placement Cell

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering on and utilizing the College Placement Management System, students and campus recruiters agree to abide by the institutional recruitment guidelines established by the College Placement Board.

2. Candidate Eligibility and Academic Integrity

All student participants are subject to the following statutory obligations:

  • Students must ensure that all entered academic records, including CGPA, 10th and 12th standard percentage marks, and active backlog counts, are accurate and verifiable against official college registrar transcripts.
  • Falsification of academic scores or resume information will lead to immediate deactivation from the placement portal and referral to the academic disciplinary committee.
  • The system's automated eligibility engine strictly enforces corporate hiring criteria. Students who do not meet defined cutoffs are disqualified from application submission.
3. Application and Interview Protocol
  • Application submission is subject to the published deadline for each drive. Late submissions cannot be accepted.
  • Once an application is shortlisted or an interview round has been scheduled, candidate withdrawal is strictly disallowed to respect corporate commitments.
  • Candidates are expected to maintain professional conduct during virtual and in-person interviews and adhere to stated joining timelines.
4. Placement Policy ("One Offer" Guideline)

In accordance with institutional placement policies, students who accept a formal recruitment offer are designated as Placed. Further participation in subsequent placement drives is governed by tiered package threshold regulations defined by the Dean of Training and Placements.

5. System Availability and Technical Specifications

The college makes no warranty regarding uninterrupted portal availability. Scheduled system maintenance will be broadcast via portal announcements in advance.
