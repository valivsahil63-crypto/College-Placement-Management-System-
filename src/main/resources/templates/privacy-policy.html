Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | College Placement and Training Cell

1. Introduction

The College Placement Management System is dedicated to maintaining the confidentiality and integrity of personal and academic data entrusted to us by students, faculty, and corporate recruitment partners. This Privacy Policy outlines the categories of data we collect, our processing objectives, and data protection measures.

2. Information We Collect

To facilitate automated recruitment eligibility and interview coordination, we collect the following records:

  • Identity Information: Full legal name, official student roll number (USN), and institutional email address.
  • Academic Credentials: Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA), 10th and 12th standard percentage marks, graduation batch year, and active backlogs.
  • Curriculum Vitae: Student-uploaded PDF resumes containing project histories, technical capabilities, and verified contact phone numbers.
  • System Logs: IP address, authentication timestamps, and administrative audit traces for security compliance.
3. Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing

Student records are processed exclusively for recruitment purposes:

  • Automated verification of academic eligibility against corporate hiring cutoffs.
  • Transmittal of candidate dossiers to prospective hiring organizations for registered campus drives.
  • Coordination of virtual or on-campus interview schedules.
  • Compilation of institutional accreditation metrics and department-wise placement statistics.
4. Data Access and Security

Access to student resumes and academic records is governed by role-based access control (RBAC). Only authenticated placement officers, system administrators, and verified recruiters have authorized access. Sensitive data fields and passwords are cryptographic hashes using industry-standard BCrypt encoders.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Academic data is retained throughout the student enrollment lifecycle and following graduation for institutional accreditation audit requirements, in compliance with academic record retention policies.

6. Inquiries and Contact Information

For questions regarding student profile records or data privacy inquiries, contact the Placement and Training Cell at placement-cell@college.edu.
